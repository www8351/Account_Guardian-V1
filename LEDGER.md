## PROJECT

AccountGuardian is a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor, written in MQL5, that guards one trading account against a daily loss.
It covers the whole account: every symbol and every source, other EAs, manual trades and the mobile app included.
The day runs from 01:00 server time. Its result is measured against the stricter of a percent limit and a currency limit, each chosen from a fixed list with a ceiling, and against a realized peak trailing floor.
On a breach it locks the account until the next day anchor, closes every open position, deletes every pending order, and keeps the account flat while locked.
A restart does not clear a lock: at boot the lock is derived again from the state file, a global variable mirror, the broker's deal history and the guardian's own sweep deals.
A raised limit does not loosen the day: a ratchet holds the stricter value until the next anchor.
An MQL5 Service sends Telegram alerts and a status message every 4 hours, and a hosted dead man check reports when its pings stop.
Customers install it on Windows with an installer that compiles the source in their own MetaEditor. It runs on a dedicated computer and cannot act while the terminal is closed.
